Welcome to the official student website for Rift Valley National Polytechnic Kuresoi Campus.

Our campus provides opportunities for students to develop technical, practical and professional skills for employment, entrepreneurship and further education.

Technology For Industrialization

ABOUT KURESOI CAMPUS

RVNP Kuresoi Campus is part of Rift Valley National Polytechnic. The campus provides technical and vocational education and training to students who want to acquire practical and professional skills.

Students are given opportunities to develop skills that can support employment, entrepreneurship and further education.

The campus offers training in different technical, business and practical areas.

OUR MISSION

To provide quality technical and vocational education and training through practical skills, innovation and technology.

What the Mission Means

The mission focuses on providing students with knowledge, practical skills and professional abilities that can help them participate effectively in the workplace and society.

OUR VISION

To provide quality education, skills and training that prepare students for employment, entrepreneurship and lifelong learning.

Our Goal

The campus aims to support students in becoming skilled, innovative and responsible members of society.

HISTORY

RVNP Kuresoi Campus is part of Rift Valley National Polytechnic and provides technical and vocational training opportunities to students in the region.

The campus supports skills development through practical training and technical education.

OUR COURSES

RVNP Kuresoi Campus provides different areas of technical and practical training.

ICT

Information and Communication Technology.

Students learn computer applications, programming, databases, networking, web development and information systems.

PLUMBING

Plumbing training focuses on installation, maintenance and repair of water supply, drainage and plumbing systems.

COSMETOLOGY

Cosmetology training develops practical skills in beauty therapy, hair care, skin care and personal grooming.

MASONRY

Masonry training covers construction skills including brickwork, blockwork, building construction and finishing.

ELECTRICAL

Electrical training provides practical skills in electrical installation, maintenance, wiring and safety.

OFFICE ADMINISTRATION

Office Administration develops skills in office management, communication, records management, computer applications and customer service.
